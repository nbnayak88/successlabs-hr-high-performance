# APH3 — Interview Simulator Blueprint

## Applied SAP SuccessFactors Performance & Goals

**Coverage:** 22 themes × 20 scenarios = **440 scenario-based interview positions**

### Simulator flow
1. Select theme or mixed assessment.
2. Present one scenario question.
3. Candidate answers using STAR.
4. Evaluate Situation, Task, Action and Result.
5. Check SAP SuccessFactors Performance & Goals product relevance.
6. Check architecture signal and measurable business outcome.
7. Ask the SME Probe as a follow-up.
8. Score and recommend a mastery level.

### Scoring dimensions
- Domain understanding
- Product/application knowledge
- Business/process reasoning
- Solution and architecture thinking
- STAR clarity
- Practical action quality
- Measurable result
- Stakeholder/consulting judgment
- Product boundary awareness
- SME depth

### Assessment modes
- Theme practice: 10 questions from one theme
- Full theme: 20 questions
- Cross-theme interview: 20 mixed questions
- Architect assessment: 30 questions weighted toward design, integration, governance, transformation and enterprise value
- Final simulation: 40 questions across all 22 themes

### Candidate progression
HR Practitioner → HR Technology Consultant → HR Tech Solution Architect → HR Transformation Architect → Trusted HR Tech Advisor

### Product boundary
APH3 owns Performance & Goals, goal management, performance reviews, continuous feedback, calibration, performance evidence, development linkage and performance insights. AGL4 owns Succession & Development; ARP5 owns Compensation & Variable Pay; AWF1 owns Employee Central.

### Mastery rule
A strong answer must explain the business context, make a defensible technology/architecture decision, describe implementation or operating action, quantify or qualify the result, and withstand the SME probe.
