### HR-APH3-B12-Q01 — Performance Support Operating Model

**Interview Question:** How would you design an operating model for ongoing Performance & Goals support?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** After go-live, every performance issue was being escalated directly to the implementation team.

**Task:** I needed to establish a sustainable support model.

**Action:** I defined L1/L2/L3 responsibilities, HR process ownership, application support, integration support, vendor escalation, SLAs and knowledge-management responsibilities.

**Result:** Support became predictable and scalable rather than dependent on project resources.

**SAP SuccessFactors Performance & Goals Example:** Employee questions were routed to appropriate HR support, configuration issues to application support and platform defects through governed escalation.

**SME Probe:** How do you decide what belongs in L1 versus L2?

### HR-APH3-B12-Q02 — Incident Management

**Interview Question:** How would you manage a critical Performance & Goals incident during an active review cycle?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Managers could not submit performance forms during a critical review deadline.

**Task:** I needed to restore business continuity quickly.

**Action:** I assessed scope and severity, established incident ownership, communicated impact, investigated the failure, applied a controlled workaround or fix and monitored recovery.

**Result:** Critical review activity resumed with controlled stakeholder communication.

**SAP SuccessFactors Performance & Goals Example:** Form submission, workflow and access were investigated across configuration, RBP and platform dependencies.

**SME Probe:** What makes an HR application incident business-critical?
