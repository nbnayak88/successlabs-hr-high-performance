### HR-APH3-B20-Q01 — From Project to Transformation
### Interview Question
How would you transform a Performance & Goals implementation from a technology project into a business transformation?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** The organization defined success as configuring and deploying the performance module.

**Task:** I needed to establish a transformation-oriented roadmap.

**Action:** I connected business strategy, performance philosophy, employee and manager journeys, process, data, technology, adoption and measurable outcomes. I defined success beyond go-live.

**Result:** The program became focused on improving performance management rather than simply deploying software.

**SAP SuccessFactors Performance & Goals Example:** I would frame the journey from goals and feedback through review, calibration, development linkage and workforce-performance insight.

**SME Probe:** What would be your first non-technical transformation metric?
