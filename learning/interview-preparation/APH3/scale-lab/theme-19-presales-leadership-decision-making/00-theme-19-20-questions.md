### HR-APH3-B19-Q01 — Shaping a Performance Transformation Opportunity
### Interview Question
How would you shape a presales opportunity when a client says they want to “modernize performance management”?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** A prospective client described modernization as replacing its existing performance tool.

**Task:** I needed to understand the transformation opportunity before proposing a solution.

**Action:** I explored business strategy, performance philosophy, employee and manager pain points, current processes, technology landscape, data, adoption and desired outcomes. I then shaped a phased target state.

**Result:** The conversation moved from product replacement to a measurable performance-transformation opportunity.

**SAP SuccessFactors Performance & Goals Example:** I would position Performance & Goals around goals, continuous feedback, performance evidence, reviews, calibration and workforce-performance insight.

**SME Probe:** What discovery question would reveal whether the client is ready for transformation?
