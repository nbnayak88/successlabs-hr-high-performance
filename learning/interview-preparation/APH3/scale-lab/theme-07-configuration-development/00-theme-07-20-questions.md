### HR-APH3-B07-Q01 — Configuration Strategy

**Interview Question:** How would you approach configuring Performance & Goals after the solution design is approved?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** A project team wanted to begin configuration immediately after requirements workshops.

**Task:** I needed to ensure configuration followed the approved architecture and process.

**Action:** I established configuration work packages for goal plans, performance forms, rating scales, workflows, permissions, calibration and reporting, with traceability to approved requirements and design decisions.

**Result:** Configuration became controlled implementation rather than ad hoc screen building.

**SAP SuccessFactors Performance & Goals Example:** Goal Management and Performance Management configuration was sequenced from foundational objects to forms, workflow, permissions and analytics.

**SME Probe:** What should be frozen before configuration starts?

### HR-APH3-B07-Q16 — Configuration Defect Diagnosis

**Interview Question:** How would you diagnose a performance-form issue reported by users?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Managers reported that a required section was missing from their form.

**Task:** I needed to determine whether the issue was configuration, permissions, workflow or data context.

**Action:** I reproduced the issue, compared affected and unaffected users, checked form/template configuration, permissions and employee context, then isolated the root cause.

**Result:** The defect was corrected without changing unrelated configuration.

**SAP SuccessFactors Performance & Goals Example:** I would inspect Performance Management form configuration and RBP before making changes.

**SME Probe:** Why should you avoid changing configuration before reproducing the issue?
