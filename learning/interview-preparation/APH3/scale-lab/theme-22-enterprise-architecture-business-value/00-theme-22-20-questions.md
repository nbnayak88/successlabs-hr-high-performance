# APH3 Theme 22 — Enterprise Architecture & Business Value

**Stable IDs:** HR-APH3-B22-Q01 → HR-APH3-B22-Q20

## HR-APH3-B22-Q01 — Performance & Goals in Enterprise Architecture
### Interview Question
Where does Performance & Goals fit within an enterprise HR architecture?
### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Performance & Goals was implemented as an isolated HR application.  
**Task:** Position it as an enterprise capability.  
**Action:** I would place it in the talent/performance domain, consume authoritative employee and organization context from Employee Central, and define governed relationships with learning, succession, compensation, analytics, identity, integration and experience capabilities.  
**Result:** Performance management becomes an integrated enterprise capability rather than a standalone application.
### SAP SuccessFactors Performance & Goals Example
Use Employee Central as core employee/organization context and connect Performance & Goals to adjacent SuccessFactors capabilities through governed interfaces.
### SME Probe
Which data should Performance & Goals own versus consume?

## HR-APH3-B22-Q04 — Architecture Principles
### Interview Question
What architecture principles would you apply to a global Performance & Goals solution?
### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Regions requested highly customized processes.  
**Task:** Balance global consistency with legitimate local needs.  
**Action:** I would establish standard-first design, authoritative data, API-led integration, security/privacy by design, experience-led design, configuration before customization and scalability by design.  
**Result:** The solution remains maintainable while supporting controlled variation.
### SAP SuccessFactors Performance & Goals Example
Prefer standard Performance & Goals capabilities and justify extensions through documented business requirements.
### SME Probe
Which principle would you refuse to compromise?

## HR-APH3-B22-Q05 — Integration Architecture and Value
### Interview Question
How does integration architecture affect Performance & Goals business value?
### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Performance data was disconnected from employee, learning and talent processes.  
**Task:** Improve decision continuity without duplicate data.  
**Action:** I would define system-of-record ownership, identity, integration contracts, data frequency, error handling and security boundaries, prioritizing integrations tied to measurable decisions.  
**Result:** Integration becomes a value enabler rather than infrastructure for its own sake.
### SAP SuccessFactors Performance & Goals Example
Connect Performance & Goals with Employee Central and relevant talent processes through supported APIs/integration services.
### SME Probe

## HR-APH3-B22-Q06 — Data Architecture
### Interview Question
What data architecture considerations are critical for Performance & Goals?
### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Systems used inconsistent employee, organization and performance definitions.  
**Task:** Establish trustworthy performance information.  
**Action:** I would define ownership, lineage, semantics, retention, access, quality rules and reporting patterns while separating transactional and analytical data.  
**Result:** Reconciliation effort falls and leadership receives consistent information.
### SAP SuccessFactors Performance & Goals Example
Align employee and organization context with Employee Central while keeping governed performance transactions within Performance & Goals.
### SME Probe
What happens when two applications both claim to be the performance system of record?

## HR-APH3-B22-Q08 — Security Architecture
### Interview Question
How should security architecture influence Performance & Goals design?
### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Performance information contained sensitive assessments.  
**Task:** Protect confidentiality while enabling legitimate access.  
**Action:** I would apply least privilege, role-based access, segregation of duties, minimization, auditability, secure integration and appropriate retention, then test access with real business scenarios.  
**Result:** Sensitive information is protected without breaking legitimate workflows.
### SAP SuccessFactors Performance & Goals Example
Design and test Performance & Goals permissions for employees, managers, HR, calibration and administrators.
### SME Probe
Why is role design alone insufficient?

## HR-APH3-B22-Q16 — Enterprise Architecture Governance
### Interview Question
How would you govern Performance & Goals changes without making delivery too slow?
### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Governance was either absent or excessive.  
**Task:** Create proportionate control.  
**Action:** I would use impact-based thresholds: routine configuration follows a lightweight path, while changes affecting data, integration, security, AI or enterprise experience receive deeper review.  
**Result:** Governance protects enterprise integrity while routine delivery remains fast.
### SAP SuccessFactors Performance & Goals Example
